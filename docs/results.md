rows are disjoint reporting lanes rather than counts of every overlapping flag.

the full local dashboard; 70 relevant ads are flagged multi-role. Same-content
copies retain distinct IDs (196 additional IDs with repeated title/body content).

The Step 7 publication retained all 322 prior analyzed jobs and added 1,456;
requirements were extracted deterministically. V3 duties abstentions remain
metadata rather than inclusion vetoes. Source evidence validates traceability,
not independent correctness. Low coverage can reflect extractor limitations or
missing explicit qualifications; it does not establish absence of requirements.

These aggregates were copied from the preserved Step 7 publication summary.
Per-ad evidence, provider receipts, local paths and original employer/contact
information are excluded. See the active inclusion policy and requirements
schema for interpretation. No API calls were made to prepare this public report.

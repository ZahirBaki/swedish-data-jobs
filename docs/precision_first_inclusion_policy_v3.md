# Precision-first v3: active automatic duties inclusion

Policy version: precision-first-v3. Approved by the user in this session for the
qualifying jobs from the final 37-job preview. Activation date: 24 October 2026.

This activates the exact substantive rules of the frozen proposal at
precision_first_inclusion_policy_v3_DRAFT.md, with its content hash recorded in
each publication's policy_activation.json. The draft and prior v1/v2 policies
remain unchanged. It does not rewrite historical classifications or grant approval
to failed, ambiguous or excluded jobs. The source-grounded deterministic evaluator
in preview_duties_v3.py is frozen to the approved preview's recorded hash.

All gates are recomputed from original collected text and saved Step 3F 1.1 outputs.
Only members of the approved preview's qualifying set that still pass every gate
are included. An expected count never forces acceptance. The publication records
source/policy/rules identities, per-job duties evidence, decisions and reasons.
Technical failures, abstentions, academic, adjacent, governance and restricted
opportunity groups remain separate. No paid calls or reclassification are permitted.

Publication approval method is automated_deterministic under precision-first-v3,
with human_reviewed=false. Never forge v1 person-audit fields or resolve original
model review flags. The earlier 108 jobs retain historical-step5-accepted-subset
provenance in additive publication metadata; they are not retroactively v3-audited.
Original records, checkpoints, caches and extraction evidence are preserved.

Deterministic requirements extraction and unique-advertisement merging feed the
latest versioned dashboard publication. Requirement denominators use accepted
analyzed jobs after filtering. Coverage remains descriptive of this selected
subset; neither confidence nor source quotations constitute independent proof,
and no human-validated accuracy claim is made.
